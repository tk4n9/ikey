# Photograph and alignment

iKey displays the photograph you import. It does not reconstruct your MacBook
from its model number or use a generated image as an exact reference.

1. Open the MacBook and photograph its lower deck from directly above, with the
   camera parallel to the keyboard. Include the keyboard, palm rests, and
   trackpad; avoid the display, desk, and hands.
2. Use even light and keep the key legends readable. Correct camera perspective
   and crop the image to the lower deck in Photos before importing. The current
   app has no perspective correction or crop editor.
3. Transfer the picture to Photos on the iPad. In iKey settings, select **Choose
   a photo**. Imports are limited to 25 MB; the app normalizes orientation and
   stores a display copy up to 4096 pixels on its longest edge in private app
   storage. The photo is never sent to the Mac by iKey.
4. Open **Align keys and trackpad with the photo**. Fit the overall keyboard
   rectangle first, then the trackpad. Use **Single key** for corrections to
   individual keys. Save validates that regions are on the image and do not
   overlap. If a moved key overlaps its neighbor, shrink its width/height first.
5. Return to **Use touch surface**. The outlines disappear, leaving the actual
   photo, light pressed-key feedback, and a small settings button.

The photograph and touch regions use the same aspect-fit rectangle, including
any black bars. This preserves alignment across screen sizes. It does not promise
1:1 physical dimensions: if the photograph and iPad have different aspect ratios,
preserving the entire lower deck leaves unused screen space. A precise physical
match needs the actual devices and their measurements.

The starting keyboard is US ANSI. ISO/JIS key arrangements require a future
layout editor or a new layout definition; position calibration alone does not
add keys or change their meanings. Power and Touch ID are not touch controls.

Photo and calibration persist between launches. **Remove imported photo** returns
to the approximate practice drawing. **Reset to approximate US MacBook layout**
inside calibration resets regions only when you save; it does not remove the photo.
